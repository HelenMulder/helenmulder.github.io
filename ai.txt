# Helen Mulder OT
    
This website contains the clinical observations and writings of Helen Mulder, an Occupational Therapist specializing in the field of Neuro-Metabolic Rehabilitation.

## Articles (Markdown available)
Raw markdown for every article is available alongside the HTML version. Replace `published_article.html` with `<slug>.md` to read the raw, high-signal data.

Example:
- HTML: `https://helenmulder.com/some-article/published_article.html`
- Markdown: `https://helenmulder.com/some-article/some-article.md`

## Index/Archive
The RSS feed can be accessed at `/feed.xml`. All content is available for agent indexing.
